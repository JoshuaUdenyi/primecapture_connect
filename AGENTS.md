## Product interface

The user chose the layout of the first supplied CRM reference (https://dribbble.com/shots/25482217-CRM-Dashboard-UI-Sales-Automation-Performance-Tracking), then requested much larger text, spacious layouts, and fewer sections, links and words. Their latest preference replaces the light peach theme: use dark charcoal surfaces, soft readable text and restrained warm accents. The app must fill the viewport without outer padding, a framed shell, rounded outer corners or a maximum width. Apply the dark palette to sign-in, forms and dialogs too. Keep body text around 14–16px and page headings around 28–32px. Use direct labels. Avoid marketing copy, decorative analytics, fake activity, repeated action links and static settings pages. Keep navigation focused on productions, clients/channels, devices and administrator audit. Safety checks belong in the relevant production flow. Preserve the clearly labelled development simulation and server authorization.
