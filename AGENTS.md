## Product interface

The user chose the first supplied CRM reference (https://dribbble.com/shots/25482217-CRM-Dashboard-UI-Sales-Automation-Performance-Tracking): light surfaces, peach/orange colors, pastel accents and rounded cards. They then explicitly requested much larger text, spacious layouts, and fewer sections, links and words. Keep body text around 14–16px and page headings around 28–32px. Use direct labels. Avoid marketing copy, decorative analytics, fake activity, repeated action links and static settings pages. Keep navigation focused on productions, clients/Pages, devices and administrator audit. Safety checks belong in the relevant production flow. Preserve the clearly labelled development simulation and server authorization.
